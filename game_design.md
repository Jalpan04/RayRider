# Game Design Document

## Game Design

The game is an open-world exploration and role-playing experience where character progression is entirely tied to the discovery and consumption of lost cultural media, replacing traditional RPG stat systems. You navigate a vast, vertical, and deliberately empty metropolis using a classic, heavy motorcycle. Traversal requires solving environmental puzzles, jumping across unfinished overpasses, and navigating precarious ramps to break into sealed-off architectural sectors like abandoned archives and hidden speakeasies.

The motorcycle also serves as a vital mobile hub and radio station. It requires scavenged parts for maintenance, and playing collected music while riding provides specific passive buffs to your character.

## Art Style

The visual direction is heavily rooted in Brutalist architecture, defined by oppressive, geometric concrete, raw gray textures, massive monolithic pillars, harsh angles, and deep shadows. The environment is deliberately sterile and monochromatic. Color is extremely rare and is used strictly as a gameplay and visual guide, existing only around the cultural artifacts you need to collect. The user interface is highly minimalist, styled like a retro-digital watch that uses blocky, glowing green elements to track fuel, time of day, and an artifact radar.

## Key Features

- **Media-Based Attribute System:** Consuming different types of media dictates your character's abilities and stats rather than leveling up traditional skill trees:
  - _Literature (Books, Poetry):_ Increases empathy and introspection, unlocking nuanced dialogue options for persuasion and conflict de-escalation.
  - _Music (Cassettes, Vinyl):_ Enhances kinetic traits and rebellion, altering combat styles to make movements faster, sharper, and more unpredictable.
  - _Cinema (Film Reels, VHS):_ Alters perception, physically changing the UI and allowing the player to spot hidden traps, clues, or alternate paths that break the laws of physics.

- **Reactive Character Progression:** As media is consumed, the character's physical behaviors dynamically shift. Idle animations, motorcycle riding posture, and dialogue style all change to reflect the dominant cultural influences you have chosen to integrate.

- **Dynamic, Build-Driven Endings:** The conclusion of the game is directly determined by the player's final personality "build" based on the media consumed. This allows for drastically different outcomes, such as leading an explosive revolution, hacking the city to quietly broadcast forbidden culture, or balancing all traits to simply escape the city.
